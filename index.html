Privacy Policy for istorya

Last Updated: October 26, 2023

This Privacy Policy describes how istorya ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our application (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

When you use istorya to connect to TikTok, we may collect the following information via TikTok's API (subject to the scopes you approve):

  • Basic Profile Information: Your TikTok username and profile picture (via user.info.basic).
  • Authentication Tokens: Access tokens required to publish content on your behalf (via video.publish).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate you and display your account details within our tool.
  • To upload and publish the video content that you explicitly create and select for publication.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not scrape or store your TikTok data beyond what is necessary to maintain the active session for posting.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your authentication tokens. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
